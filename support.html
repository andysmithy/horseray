Getting a good read

  • Side-on, with the whole horse and its legs in view, gives the fullest result.
  • Wait for "Good angle" on the camera before you tap.
  • Head-on, distant or blocked shots come back as "No verdict" rather than a guess. A borderline read means the shot showed only part of the horse.
  • If a scan fails for lack of signal, your photo is kept and no scan is used. Tap Try again, or retry later from History.
