Horse Ray privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Horse Ray is an iPhone app that scores photos and short videos of racehorses. This page explains what it collects, why, and what you can do about it. "We" means Andrew Smithyman, who makes Horse Ray. Contact: andysmithy@icloud.com.

What we collect

Photos and videos you scan. When you analyse a scan, a few still frames from it are sent to our server and passed to Anthropic's Claude service, which produces the result. We don't keep those frames unless you turn on sharing (see Scan bank below). Clips you film stay on your phone, and are saved to your Photos only if you leave that setting on.

Scan results. The result of every scan is kept on our server with your account, whether or not sharing is on. That means the scores and written observations, plus the venue, race, horse number and any name you typed, but not the photo. We keep them so plan limits work and so results can be checked later.

Your account. You don't need to sign up to use the free tier. The first time you scan, the app makes an anonymous account with no name or email, so your free scans can be counted.

If you choose to sign in with an email address or with Sign in with Apple, we keep that email (or the private relay address Apple gives us) and an account ID, so your plan, passes and race credits carry over if you change phones. Your scan history stays on your phone.

Purchases. We record which plan, pass or race pack you have, using Apple's purchase records. We never see your card details.

Location. When you open the camera or race details, the app asks whether to use your location to find the racecourse and the weather. If you allow it, the nearest racecourse is worked out on your phone from a built-in list, and your location, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, for the track weather. If you don't, you pick the course from a list. Your location isn't sent to us, linked to your account or stored.

Usage counts. We count scans and races per day so plan limits work. To stop free scans being abused, we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your network address for up to two days. Once it's switched on, we also use Apple's DeviceCheck, which tells us only whether this phone has already had a free account this month.

We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your data.

Who else handles it

  • Anthropic (Claude) analyses the scan frames. Under its commercial terms, data sent through its API isn't used to train its models.
  • Supabase hosts our server, database and storage, in Sydney, Australia.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather reading described above.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and purchases.

Scan bank

Before your first scan, the app asks "Help improve Horse Ray?" and explains that, if you say yes, the photos you scan are kept privately and compared with race results. No and Yes are offered equally, and saying No doesn't stop you scanning. Sharing stays off unless you say Yes, and you can change your answer any time in Settings › Scan bank. When it's on, the photos you analyse are kept in Horse Ray's private storage beside their results, so they can be compared with race results later. They aren't shown to anyone. Switching sharing off deletes the photos you've shared, and so does deleting your account.

Keeping and deleting your data

Scans saved in the app stay on your phone until you delete them there. To delete your account, go to Settings › Account › Delete account. That removes your account, the scan results stored with it, and any shared photos from our server. Subscriptions are managed separately in your Apple ID settings. You can also email us to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted.

Age

Horse Ray is for adults (18+) and isn't directed at children.

Changes and contact
